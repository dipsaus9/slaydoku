## Description

<!-- SECTION:DESCRIPTION:BEGIN -->
Outcome: owner decision 2026-10-09. (1) Simpshouse puzzles use the same sizes and rules as every other day (no more 12x12 special; boards 6x6 to 9x9, mostly 7x7 and 8x8 per SLAY-22), so the theme and its room layouts must work on small boards; the one-off day 2026-10-14 stays a Simpshouse day but is no longer frozen: SLAY-18.10 regenerates it with the new rules. (2) Simpshouse comes back on the 1st of every month (a day-of-month rule in the calendar, in addition to 2026-10-14), with priority over the fall, christmas and halloween windows: 2026-11-01, 2026-12-01 and 2027-01-01 are the dates in the delivered range. (3) The Simpshouse cast always contains Romy and Dennis; first letters stay unique (so Ruben and Ralph never appear with Romy, Duncan never with Dennis); the other suspects are a random pick from the pool with genders balanced within 1, deterministic per day. The schedule files are not changed here: SLAY-18.10 regenerates once at the end.
